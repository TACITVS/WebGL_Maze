Palette

Every colour in the game comes from these ramps and nowhere else — sprites, masonry, props, HUD and menus. Each runs dark to light in steps that shift hue as they climb: shadows lean cool, highlights lean warm, saturation peaks in the middle. Walls are drawn from steps 0–3, creatures from 2–4, so a monster is always lighter than the stone behind it.

Cast

Nothing down here is an animal. Every creature is a bound construct of the same three materials: a body of pale bone, clamped with bands of corroded verdigris, animated by an ember core. The core is drawn on its own layer so it can glow — which makes the aim point and the attack telegraph the same pixel. Light falls from the upper left on all of them.
