NEXUS DEPTHS

PROCEDURAL 3D DUNGEONS · RAW WEBGL · NO ASSETS

Four playable pages, all client-side. Nothing is bundled, nothing is downloaded: every sprite is painted at boot with fillRect, every sound is synthesised at runtime, and the renderer is raw WebGL with no engine underneath it.

THE GAME

Nexus Depths

A bullet heaven in first person. Your weapons fire themselves; your job is where you stand and where you look. Kill the floor's quota to crack open the way down, then choose: descend to safety, or stay and grow stronger while the spawn rate keeps climbing. Every level deals you three procedurally generated capabilities — 2,304 base weapon identities before rarity — so no two runs hand you the same build.

PLAY
WASD move · Shift sprint · Mouse look · LMB surge · RMB/Space blast · 123 choose
